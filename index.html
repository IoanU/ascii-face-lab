Before you start.

A few useful things to know about fitting, editing, and sharing a portrait.

How many photos do I need?

One clear front photo is enough to start. A matching profile and a three-quarter photo give the fit more information about depth and head shape. You can add up to eight views around the head.

Are my photos uploaded?

Photos are analyzed in your browser and are not sent to a photo-processing server. The detector downloads its model files and runtimes when you first use photo fitting.

What happens to angles I do not photograph?

With “Estimate features from missing angles” enabled, the studio fills missing views using the front photo, symmetry, and the reference head. These areas are estimates; extra photographs help constrain them.

Will it work offline?

The reference model, manual sculpting, and exported HTML viewer work without a detector download. Photo fitting needs internet access to load its external runtimes and models. Opening an export does not require the editor.

Are my edits saved automatically?

Edits stay in the current editor tab. Export an HTML model before closing or reloading the page. Undo and redo work during the editing session.
