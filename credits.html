The people behind the reference.

The demonstrations use the reference scan included in the studio. They do not use uploaded user photographs.

Reference head

“Infinite” 3D Head Scan by Lee Perry-Smith / Infinite Realities, adapted for ASCII Face Lab. Shared under Creative Commons Attribution 3.0. The mesh is available through InfiniteScan. The web demonstrations and their exports preserve this attribution.

Photo-analysis components

Face landmarks and head segmentation use MediaPipe. Face-part parsing uses BiSeNet ResNet18 weights by Yakhyokhuja Valikhujaev, run through ONNX Runtime.

The bundled parser's MIT license is included. MediaPipe and ONNX Runtime are loaded from external hosts when photo detection starts; their Apache 2.0 and MIT notices are provided for reference.

Type

Manrope by the Manrope Project Authors is used for the interface. IBM Plex Mono by IBM is used for ASCII portraits and code. Both are self-hosted under the SIL Open Font License: Manrope license and IBM Plex Mono license.

About your photos

Your chosen photographs are processed locally in the browser. No photo-upload service, advertising tracker, or analytics script is included in this project.
